ArenaSwap uses chrome.alarms to keep checking scores on schedule while no game is close to starting. Between games, sometimes for days in an off-season, the extension checks each league for scheduled games only every few minutes to half an hour, longer than a Manifest V3 service worker stays running. It sets one alarm for the time of its next check so the service worker starts again then, and does not check any sooner than its own schedule would. The alarm carries no data and triggers no network request beyond the score check that was already due.
