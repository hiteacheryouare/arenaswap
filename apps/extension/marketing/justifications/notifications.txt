ArenaSwap uses chrome.notifications to tell the user why their active tab just changed. The extension creates exactly two notifications, both immediately after a tab switch has completed: one when it switches to a more exciting game, carrying that game's current score, the venue, and the reason the switch was triggered; and one when every game the user is tracking has gone quiet and they have been parked on the fallback tab they designated as their Standby Stream. Because the extension's purpose is to change the active tab while the user is not looking at the popup, a notification is the only way to explain a switch the user did not initiate. A single toggle in the extension's settings controls them, and no notification is created while it is off.
