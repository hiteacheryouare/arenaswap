ArenaSwap uses chrome.notifications to alert the user when a tab switch is about to occur, when a favorite team's game becomes the most exciting one, and when a critical late-game moment is detected (e.g., final two minutes of a close game). These notifications are essential to the extension's "set it and forget it" use case. The user is not actively watching the popup, so the notification is the only way to surface the moment when manual attention is needed.
