ArenaSwap uses chrome.notifications to tell the user why their active tab just changed. The extension creates four kinds of notification, all under one notification id so each replaces the last. Three come immediately after a tab switch has completed: one when it switches to a more exciting game, carrying that game's current score, the venue, and the reason the switch was triggered; one when every game the user is tracking has gone quiet and they have been parked on the fallback tab they designated as their Standby Stream; and one when the user is moved to their Multiview tab, naming the games on screen. The fourth comes just before a switch: when the extension holds a switch for a moment at a natural break in play, it says which game is up next and why. Because the extension's purpose is to change the active tab while the user is not looking at the popup, a notification is the only way to explain a switch the user did not initiate. A single toggle in the extension's settings controls them, and no notification is created while it is off.
