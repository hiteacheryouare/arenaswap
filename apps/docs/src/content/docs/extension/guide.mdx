The popup shows what's on now. The Guide shows the rest of the day, so you know when to clear the couch.

## Open the Guide

Click the calendar icon labeled **Guide** in the popup's top bar. It opens in its own browser tab, so you can leave it next to your games.

## Read the timeline

Each row is a game, grouped by league, with the hours across the top.

- A bar runs from the start of the game to roughly when it should end.
- A line marks the current time, and today's page opens scrolled to it.
- An upcoming game shows its start time and channel. A live game shows the score and clock. A finished game shows its score and **Final**.
- A filled star on a bar means one of your teams is playing.
- Hover over a bar for the matchup and start time.

You'll only see the leagues you've turned on in Settings.

## Look at other days

Use **Previous day** and **Next day** to move one day at a time. Days with no games are skipped.

How far ahead you can go is **Days ahead**, under Game List in Settings. It runs from 1 to 14 days and starts at 7. The Guide always shows at least 3 days. Games that finished in the last day stay on the grid.

## Find the best time to watch

**Best time to watch** is on by default. It shades the stretch of the day with the most games on at once. Games near the finish count for more than games just starting, and so do your favorite teams.

The header spells it out, like "3 games · 1 of yours". Turn the switch off to hide the shading.

## Open a game

Click any bar and the game opens in a panel on the right. [Game details](/arenaswap/docs/extension/game-details/) covers what's in it.

- Click another bar to swap games, and **Close** or Escape to put the panel away.
- Before a game starts, star either team. The star shows on every bar for that team.
- Add a **Game Boost** to a game you don't want to miss. Do it before kickoff and ArenaSwap starts it with a head start.

To link a game to a tab, use the popup. The Guide can't do that.
