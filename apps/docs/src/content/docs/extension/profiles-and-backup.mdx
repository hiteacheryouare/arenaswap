A playoff night and a Sunday of fantasy football want different setups. Profiles let you keep both and switch in a click. Backups let you take a setup to another browser.

## Make a profile

Everyone starts with one profile, **main**.

1. Open Settings, then **Profiles & Backup**.
2. Click **New profile** and type a name.
3. Click **Copy main** to start from your current settings, or **Start fresh** for the defaults. (The button shows the name of your current profile.)

Two profiles can't share a name. A profile holds your settings and your Fantasy roster. The new one becomes your current profile.

## Switch profiles

Click a profile's name in the **Profiles** list. A checkmark marks the one in use. Your leagues, favorite teams and scoring mode change right away.

## Rename, export or delete

Click the three dots next to a profile.

- **Rename** lets you pick a new name.
- **Export** opens the backup page with that profile ready to save.
- **Delete** removes the profile, along with its settings and Fantasy roster. Export it first if you want a copy.

You can't delete the profile you're using. Switch to another one first.

## Keep your setup in sync

Under **Syncs With Your Browser**, ArenaSwap lists what your current profile holds: leagues on, favorite teams, scoring mode and your Fantasy roster. That all follows you to other computers when sync is on in your browser. Other profiles stay put.

## Save a backup

1. Click **Back up or restore…**. It opens in a new tab.
2. If you have more than one profile, pick one from the **Profile** menu.
3. Click **Download backup**.

The file's name has the profile and today's date, like `arenaswap-settings-main-2026-11-03.json`.

## Use a backup

Open **Back up or restore…** in the browser you're moving to. If you're still in first-time setup, click **Have a backup file? Skip setup and import it**.

1. Drop the file onto **Drop a backup here**, or click **Choose a file**.
2. Compare the file's setup with yours.
3. Pick one:
   - **Add as a new profile** keeps everything you have and adds the file's setup beside it.
   - **Replace** swaps every setting in your current profile for the ones in the file. If the backup was taken in Demo mode, you'll be back in Demo mode.
4. When you see "is all moved in," click **Close tab**.

Picked the wrong file? **Choose another file** starts over.

If something goes wrong:

- "…held that file up to the light and couldn't make out a word": the file is damaged. Save a fresh backup.
- "…a perfectly good file, just not one of ours": it didn't come from ArenaSwap. Pick one you saved.
- "This file came from a newer ArenaSwap": update the extension, then try again.
- "That didn't save": try again.
