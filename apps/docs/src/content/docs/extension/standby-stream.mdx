Some nights none of your games are worth watching at once: blowouts, a rain delay, halftime. Standby Stream is a backup stream for those stretches, like a highlight show, a pregame show, or a whip-around channel.

## Set it up

1. Open your backup stream in a tab and leave it open.
2. In Settings, open **Standby Stream** and turn on **Enable Standby Stream**.
3. Pick your tab from the **Standby tab** dropdown.

If the dropdown still says **Select a tab**, nothing happens when none of your games are exciting.

The first time you turn it on, a short two-step guide shows the same steps. Tap **Got it** on the second screen when you're done.

## Choose when standby starts

**Standby below** is a slider from 0 to 100, set to 20 by default. Slide it up and ArenaSwap switches to standby sooner, while games are still mildly interesting. Slide it down and ArenaSwap keeps your games on screen longer before it gives up on them.

## What to expect

When every game you've assigned a tab to drops below that line, ArenaSwap switches to your standby tab. When any one of them climbs back over it, it switches you back.

If you click over to another tab, ArenaSwap moves you to your best game. If nothing has heated up by the end of the cooldown, it brings you back to the standby tab. To browse without being moved at all, turn off **Enable auto-switching**.

Standby Stream only watches games you've assigned a tab to. With no game tabs assigned, it has nothing to fall back from.
