Demo mode gives you practice games, so you can see ArenaSwap switch tabs when nothing real is on. It's handy in the off-season, or any time you want to try it before trusting it with your browser.

## Turn it on

1. Open Settings and choose **Switching**.
2. Under **Demo**, turn on **Demo mode**.

The game list fills with practice games from several leagues. They show up whether or not those leagues are turned on, and their scores keep moving. Some build a lead. Some stage a comeback.

<Demo name='toggle' setting='setup.demoMode' />

## Try a switch

Assign a tab to two or three demo games, the same way you would for real ones. ArenaSwap switches, mutes and unmutes your tabs as the games heat up. [Watching multiple games](/arenaswap/docs/extension/watching-multiple-games/) has the steps.

It's also a good time to try the sliders in [How to control when ArenaSwap switches tabs](/arenaswap/docs/extension/switching-and-sensitivity/). You can see what they do right away.

**Pretend it is** appears once Demo mode is on. It only changes the holiday decorations.

## Turn it off

Switch **Demo mode** back off. The practice games disappear and your real games come back.
