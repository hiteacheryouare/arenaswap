The popup shows what's on right now. The Guide shows the rest of the day, so you know when to clear the couch.

## Open the Guide

Click the calendar icon in the popup's top bar, the one labeled **Guide**. It opens in a new browser tab, so you can leave it next to your games.

Scores update on their own while the tab is open.

## Read the timeline

Each row is a game. Rows are grouped by league, and the hours run across the top.

- A bar starts at kickoff and runs to where the game is expected to end. A live game's bar stretches or shrinks as play goes on. A finished game ends where it actually ended.
- On today's page, a line marks the current time, and the Guide opens scrolled to it.
- A game that hasn't started shows its start time and TV network. A live game shows its score and the clock. A finished game shows its score and **Final**.
- A filled star on a bar means one of your teams is playing.
- Hover over a bar to see the matchup and start time.

The Guide lists the leagues you've turned on in Settings and nothing else.

## Page through the days

The arrows at the top of the page, **Previous day** and **Next day**, move one day at a time. The Guide skips days with no games, and the arrows go gray at either end.

How far ahead you can page follows **Days ahead**, under Game List in Settings. It runs from 1 to 14 days and defaults to 7. The Guide always reaches at least 3 days, even when Days ahead is lower. The Guide uses this setting even if **Show upcoming games** is off, but the slider only appears when that switch is on.

Games that finished in the last day or so stay on the grid, so yesterday's page is still there.

## Find the best time to watch

The **Best time to watch** switch sits in the header and is on by default. It shades the stretch of the day when the most games are on at once. Games in their closing stretch count for more than games just starting, and a game with one of your teams counts for more again, in proportion to your **Favorite team boost**.

The header spells it out: the hours, the number of games, and how many of them are yours, like "3 games · 1 of yours". On today's page, the band only covers what's left of the day. Turn the switch off to hide the shading and the sentence.

## Open a game

Click any bar and the game opens in a panel on the right. It's the same screen you get from a card in the popup, so [Game details](/arenaswap/docs/extension/game-details/) covers everything in it.

- Click another bar to swap games without closing the panel.
- Press Escape or click **Close** to put it away.
- Before a game starts, you can star either team from the panel. The star shows up on every bar for that team.
- Set a **Game Boost** to add points to that game's PowerScore. Do it before kickoff and the game starts with the head start.

The panel has no tab picker. To assign a game to a tab, use the popup.
