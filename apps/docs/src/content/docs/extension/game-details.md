A card gives you the score. The detail screen gives you the argument for why you should be watching it.

## Open it

Click a game card in the popup. From the keyboard, the first Tab stop on a card opens it. In the [Guide](/arenaswap/docs/extension/guide/), click a game's bar and the same screen opens in a side panel.

Click **Back** to return to the list. In the Guide, click **Close**.

## Switch between tabs

A strip under the score splits the screen up. Which tabs show depends on the game:

- **Overview** is always there.
- **Matchup** appears before a game starts, when there's form, stats or injuries to show.
- **Box Score** appears once a game is live or final and there are numbers to show.
- **Standings** appears when the league has a table for it.

If the strip is too narrow for all of them, the extras fold into a **More** menu.

## See the PowerScore breakdown

On a live game, **Overview** has a **PowerScore Breakdown**. If you've picked a scoring mode other than Classic, the heading carries its name. It lists each Signal (Closeness, Late-game, Momentum, Lead changes, Comeback) with the points it adds, then the Boosts and Penalties, then the **Final PowerScore**. A plain-words line under the total says what's driving the score right now.

A Signal you've turned off shows **Off**. [Scoring modes](/arenaswap/docs/extension/scoring-modes/) covers how the modes differ.

A game in **Halftime**, an **Intermission** or a delay scores 0 until play resumes. Finished games don't show a PowerScore at all.

## Read the charts

Charts sit under the breakdown and fill in as the game goes:

- **PowerScore Over Time**
- **Game Score Over Time**
- **Win Probability**
- **PowerScore Components Over Time**
- **Lead Over Time**, only for a game scored in Blowouts mode

A finished game only shows its charts if ArenaSwap watched the whole game.

## Check the game info

**Game Info** lists where to watch, the venue and weather, and for finished games the end time and attendance, where the league reports them. Before a game starts, it can also link to **Tickets**.

The **Line** appears only if you've turned on **Show betting & odds** in Settings. Weather follows your **Temperature unit** setting.

## Follow the game

What shows up on a live game changes with the sport:

- **Latest Play** shows what just happened. Soccer has no play-by-play, so it shows **Red Cards** instead.
- **Due Up** shows the next hitters between half-innings in baseball.
- Football shows down and distance. Basketball shows timeouts and which team is in the bonus. Playoff series show a series tracker.

## Get ready before kickoff

A game that hasn't started has a card headed **Get Ready for Kickoff**, or tip-off, puck drop or first pitch, depending on the sport. It holds the Game Boost field. In the popup, it also holds the picker that opens the game in a tab when it starts.

Below it:

- **Probable Pitchers** in baseball and softball, or **Probable Goalies** in hockey.
- **Team Leaders** with each team's top performers.
- The **Matchup** tab: **Recent form**, **Season stats** and **Injuries**. Which stats show depends on the sport.

Star either team with the star next to its crest. Starred teams get a PowerScore bonus, as [favorite teams](/arenaswap/docs/extension/favorite-teams/) explains. Stars on live and finished games live on the card in the list.

## Check the box score and standings

**Box Score** opens with the score by inning, quarter, period or half, then team stats and player lines. The columns follow the sport: batting and pitching in baseball, passing, rushing and receiving in football, skaters and goaltending in hockey.

**Standings** shows the league table with the two teams in this game highlighted.

## Add a Game Boost

**Game Boost** adds points to this game's PowerScore, so ArenaSwap favors it when it decides where to switch. Type a whole number from 0 to 100. Anything higher becomes 100, and 0 removes the boost.

- It's the only thing that can push a PowerScore past 100. The breakdown then reads like "125 (base max 100)".
- It sits on live and upcoming games. Finished games don't have it.
- It's counted as **Game boost** in the breakdown. While play is stopped, the score reads 0 and the boost waits.
- Your boosts last until you close your browser.

Use it for the game you won't miss, whatever the PowerScore says.
