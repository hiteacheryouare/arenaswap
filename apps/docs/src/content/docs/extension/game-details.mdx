Click any game to see why it's ranked where it is, and what's riding on it.

## Open a game

Click a game card in the popup. In the [Guide](/arenaswap/docs/extension/guide/), click a game's bar and it opens in a side panel. Click **Back** to return to the list, or **Close** in the Guide.

A row of tabs sits under the score: **Overview** is always there, **Matchup** shows up before a game starts, **Box Score** once it's under way, and **Standings** when your league has a table. If they don't all fit, the rest go under **More**.

## See why a game is ranked so high

Open a live game and look at the **PowerScore Breakdown** on **Overview**. It shows what's making the game worth watching:

- **Closeness**: how tight the score is.
- **Late-game**: how close the end is, while it's still anyone's game.
- **Momentum**: whether one team is on a run.
- **Lead changes**: how often the lead has swapped lately.
- **Comeback**: whether the team behind is climbing back in.

Each one shows its points, followed by any Boosts (extra points, like for your favorite team) and Penalties (points off, like during a commercial break), and then the **Final PowerScore**. The line under the total says what's driving it right now.

<Demo name='breakdown' />

A game paused for **Halftime**, an **Intermission**, or a delay shows 0 until play starts again. Finished games don't get a PowerScore. [Scoring modes](/arenaswap/docs/extension/scoring-modes/) covers how to change what counts.

## Follow how the game's going

The charts under the breakdown fill in as play goes on. **PowerScore Over Time** shows when the game got good, and **Win Probability** shows how the odds have swung. There are also charts for the score and the lead.

<Demo name='charts' />

Live games add more, depending on the sport. **Latest Play** shows what just happened, and in baseball **Due Up** shows who's coming to the plate. Football shows down and distance, and basketball shows timeouts and bonus. Playoff games show the series score.

## Get ready before the game starts

A game that hasn't started has a **Get Ready for Kickoff** card (tip-off, puck drop, or first pitch, depending on the sport). Here you can add a Game Boost and, in the popup, pick the tab to open the game in.

Below it you'll find **Probable Pitchers** or **Probable Goalies** where they apply, **Team Leaders**, and the **Matchup** tab with **Recent form**, **Season stats**, and **Injuries**. To follow a team, tap the star next to its crest. [Favorite teams](/arenaswap/docs/extension/favorite-teams/) explains what that does.

## Check the box score and standings

**Box Score** has the score period by period, team stats, and player lines for the sport you're watching. **Standings** shows the league table with these two teams highlighted.

## Find where to watch

**Game Info** lists the channel or service, the venue, and the weather. Finished games also show attendance. Upcoming games can link to **Tickets**.

The betting line only shows if you've turned on **Show betting & odds** in Settings.

## Add a Game Boost

Want a game on screen no matter what the score says? **Game Boost** adds points to its PowerScore so ArenaSwap picks it more often. Type a number from 0 to 100, and use 0 to take it off.

<Demo name='game-boost' />

The Game Boost field is on live and upcoming games. It's the one thing that can push a PowerScore above 100, and the breakdown shows it as "Game boost".
