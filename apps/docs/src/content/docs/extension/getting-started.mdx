Set ArenaSwap up once, open two games, and it moves you to whichever one is better. It takes a few minutes.

## Install it and pick your leagues

Add ArenaSwap from the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, or the Edge Add-ons store, then click its toolbar icon. A short setup walks you through the rest.

The first screen is a welcome. Click **Got it**. If you've used ArenaSwap on another computer, **Have a backup file? Skip setup and import it** brings your settings over. [Profiles and backup](/arenaswap/docs/extension/profiles-and-backup/) covers the file.

On **Which sports do you watch?**, check the leagues you follow. NBA, NFL, NHL, and MLB are checked to start. Each sport has an **All** box if you want the whole group. Click **Next**. You can change this list in Settings any time.

## Star your teams

On **Pick Your Teams**, tap a team to star it. Starred teams get a bump when ArenaSwap picks a game, so their games win more switches. [Favorite teams](/arenaswap/docs/extension/favorite-teams/) has the details.

Click **Done**, or **Skip** if you'd rather not pick yet.

You'll land on **You're all set!** Click **Take the tour** for a quick walkthrough, or **Jump right in**. The **?** button at the top replays the tour later.

## Open two live games

Start two games in two separate browser tabs, on whatever you already stream them from. No two live games right now? Turn on [Demo mode](/arenaswap/docs/extension/demo-mode/) and use its scripted ones.

## Link each tab to its game

Open ArenaSwap's popup. On each game's card, use the **Assign a tab** dropdown to pick the tab you opened for that game.

Already have the streams open? A **These Tabs Look Like Games** banner may offer to match them for you. Click **Take a look** to check its guesses.

<Demo name='popup' tabs />

Linked games move up to **Active Tabs** at the top of the list. ArenaSwap only ever switches between tabs you've linked.

## Watch it switch

Leave both tabs open and watch the game. When the other one gets more exciting, ArenaSwap takes you to it, unmutes it, and mutes the one you left. With **Switch notifications** on, which is the default, you also get a short note about what happened and why.
