Star a team and ArenaSwap leans toward its games. Close games still beat blowouts, but when two games are about equally good, your team gets the call.

## Star a team

You can star teams while setting up, on **Pick Your Teams**. After that, star or unstar from any of these:

- The star next to a team's name on a game card.
- The star next to a team's crest on an upcoming game's detail screen.
- Settings, under **Favorite Teams**, where you can search every team in your leagues.

## What a star does

Your teams' games get extra points toward being the one ArenaSwap switches to. If both teams in a game are starred, it gets the extra points twice.

Your teams' games also sit at the top of the list, above games that are more exciting but belong to nobody you follow. With **Group games by league** on, they sit at the top of their league instead.

To change how big the bump is, adjust **Favorite team boost** under Scoring in Settings. It starts at 10 points.

## Pick your rivals

Some teams you watch to see win. Others, you watch to see lose. In Settings under **Favorite Teams**, open the **Rivals** tab and tap any team you can't stand.

Rivals count in the Blowouts [scoring mode](/arenaswap/docs/extension/scoring-modes/). There, a game where a rival is down by more than two scores goes above every other game. ArenaSwap switches to it the way it switches to any game, once it clears your Sensitivity gap. A game a rival is winning drops further the bigger its lead.

A team can be a favorite or a rival, not both. Picking one on the other list moves it over.
