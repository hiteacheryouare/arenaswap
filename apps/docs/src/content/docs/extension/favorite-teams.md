Starring a team doesn't filter your game list down to just that team. It nudges ArenaSwap toward that team's games when ArenaSwap decides what deserves your tab. Favoriting isn't a one-time setup step either. You can add or remove favorites any time.

## Star a team

You'll get a first pass at favoriting during setup, on the **Pick your teams** screen. After setup, star or unstar a team from any of these:

- The star icon next to a team's name on any game card.
- The star icon next to a team's crest on a game's pre-game screen.
- Settings → **Favorite Teams**, where you can search every team in your leagues.

Both use the same star, filled when a team is favorited and outlined when it isn't.

## What favoriting does

Each favorited team in a game adds points to that game's PowerScore. If both teams in a matchup are favorites, the boost applies twice. **Favorite team boost**, under Scoring in Settings, sets the amount per team and defaults to 10 points.

Favorited games also get a placement bump that has nothing to do with PowerScore. A game with a favorited team pins to the top of its section on the main screen, regardless of score.

A game at halftime, in an intermission, or delayed scores 0, so it gets no boost and can't win your tab on your team's name alone. It stays pinned in the list.
