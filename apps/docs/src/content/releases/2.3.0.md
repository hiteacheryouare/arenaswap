The Brewers won the World Series. LeBron's Sixers are 8-1, and he'd like everyone to know it's too early to talk about the Finals. Miami is No. 1 in the first College Football Playoff rankings, Kentucky is sixth (in football), and women's college basketball tipped off this week.

Thanksgiving is three Thursdays away, and somebody at your table is already scouting the couch seat closest to the TV.

So we brought the decorations down from the attic early, and a few new ones came down with them.

Here's what's hanging.

## Stockings for Christmas

Football on Christmas Eve, the NBA and NFL on Christmas Day, and soccer on Boxing Day get a gold garland along the bottom of the scoreboard. Each team's stocking hangs from it, in that team's colors with its crest on the leg.

The stockings fill as the score does. Whoever's ahead has the fullest one: a gift, a candy cane and an ornament. At the final, the loser's stocking gets coal. A draw gets nobody coal. A shootout loss does.

## Bunting for baseball

Milwaukee spent October under bunting and came out of it with its first World Series in 57 years. Baseball's away until spring, but ours is already pressed. Three pleated fans of red, white and blue hang under the scoreboard for every team's home opener series (its first three home games of the regular season) and for every postseason game.

## Fireworks for the Fourth

On the Fourth of July, every run in an MLB game and every goal in an MLS game sets off fireworks over the scoreboard in the scoring team's colors. Blue Jays games get the same on Canada Day. A three-run homer is three bursts.

## The leaves got raked

Football still drops leaves all Thanksgiving week, this year's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game included, but now they're actual leaf shapes, tumbling on the way down into a raked pile at the bottom of the screen that grows as the game goes on. Snow (when it's really snowing at the game) and the December lights are right where you left them.

## Every decoration gets its own switch

Find them under **Holiday decorations** in **Settings > Display**: Snow, Lights, Leaves, Christmas, Bunting and Fireworks. They all start on.

Can't wait until December? Neither could we.

1. Open **Settings > Demo mode** and turn on **Demo mode**
2. Pick **Christmas Day**, **Boxing Day** or **Fourth of July** under **Pretend it is**
3. Open the NFL or NBA game for Christmas, the Premier League game for Boxing Day, or an MLB game for the Fourth

## Stocking stuffers

- **Women's college basketball is back on the list.** It was showing no games at all, which is a rough look for opening week
- **Every college team shows up in the favorite team pickers.** Plenty of them were missing
- **Switch notifications stopped piling up.** Each new one replaces the last
- **Full screen stays full screen** through a switch, and a game in another browser window comes to the front when ArenaSwap switches to it
- Turning a league off takes its live games off the list right away
- Standby Stream waits out your switch cooldown before pulling you off a game you picked yourself, and parking on standby no longer starts a cooldown, so a game that heats back up gets you back on the next update
- Changing a game boost no longer nudges every live game toward the clock stall penalty
- A game with less than a second on the clock is no longer treated as over
- The Box score and Standings tabs stay put at tip-off and at the final instead of kicking you back to Overview
- Postponed games stopped counting down to a start that isn't happening
- The list stopped calling it a quiet night when it actually couldn't load, and stopped going blank when your assigned games had left it
- Leaving the popup open no longer brings back tabs ArenaSwap had already let go
