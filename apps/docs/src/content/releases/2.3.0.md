The decorations came out early in 2.1, and the scoreboard has been fully committed to the bit ever since. This release adds the rest of the box: stockings for Christmas, bunting for baseball, and fireworks for the Fourth. Your game screen now has a better wardrobe than most of your relatives.

## The decorations grew up

Everything below lives on a game's detail screen. Turn the whole set on or off with **Holiday decorations** in **Settings > Display**, or flip each one on its own: **Snow**, **Lights**, **Leaves**, **Christmas**, **Bunting** and **Fireworks**. All of them start on.

- **Leaves:** Thanksgiving week football got a redraw. The leaves have real leaf shapes now and tumble on the way down, and they land in a raked pile at the foot of the screen that gets bigger as the game goes on
- **Christmas:** football on Christmas Eve, NBA and NFL games on Christmas Day, and soccer on Boxing Day. A gold garland runs along the bottom edge of the scoreboard with a bauble in each team's color at both ends. Under it, each team hangs a stocking in its colors with its crest on it
- **Bunting:** MLB only. Three pleated red, white and blue fans hang under the scoreboard for a team's home opener series (its first three home games of the regular season) and for every postseason game
- **Fireworks:** MLB and MLS games on the Fourth of July, and Blue Jays games on Canada Day. Every run or goal sets off fireworks over the scoreboard in the scoring team's colors, one burst per run, so a three-run homer is three. Up to four go off at once

Stockings fill up as the score does. The team in front has the fullest one, with up to three treats: a gift, a candy cane or an ornament. When the game ends, the losing team's stocking gets coal. A draw gives nobody coal. A shootout loss does.

Snow and lights work the way they did in 2.2. Snow falls when it's actually snowing at the game (never under a dome), and the lights hang across the top all December and flash in your favorite team's colors when they score.

Want to see them in July? Switch on **Demo mode** in **Settings > Demo mode**, and its **Pretend it is** picker now has Christmas Day, Boxing Day and Fourth of July next to the real date, Thanksgiving week and December.

## Fixes

- Women's college basketball showed no games at all. It does now
- Many college teams were missing from the favorite team pickers. They're all there
- Switch notifications piled up in your system's notification center. Each new one now replaces the last
- If you're watching in full screen, a switch now keeps you there. A game sitting in another browser window also now comes to the front when ArenaSwap switches to it
- Turning a league off removes its live games right away, instead of leaving them listed and switchable
- Standby Stream no longer pulls you off a game you picked yourself before your switch cooldown runs out. Parking on standby also no longer starts a cooldown, so a game that heats back up gets you back on the next update
- Changing a game boost no longer nudges every live game toward the clock stall penalty
- A clock with under a second left no longer reads as zero, so a one-possession game at the buzzer isn't treated as decided
- The Box score and Standings tabs no longer kick you back to Overview when a game tips off or goes final
- A postponed game no longer counts down to its old start time
- The game list no longer says it's a quiet night when it actually failed to load, and no longer goes blank when tabs are still assigned but their games have left the list
- Leaving the popup open no longer brings back tabs ArenaSwap had already let go
