The NFL is finally fully underway, the AL East is a literal warzone, and Pat McAfee is back to screaming his head off every Saturday morning (WE ARE SO BACK). 

But this unfortunately made this a bad week for the part of the extension that tells you what's on next to go blank.

If Up Next has been empty for you, that's the bug, and it wasn't your leagues or your settings. Our sources stopped answering us when we asked for a whole week of schedule in one request. Live games were never affected.

2.1.1 asks for the schedule a day at a time instead, which our sources are still perfectly happy to answer.
