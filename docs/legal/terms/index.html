It is not a streaming service

ArenaSwap does not host, rebroadcast, decrypt, unlock or provide access to any game. It switches between browser tabs you opened yourself, on services you are entitled to watch. Whether you can watch a given game is a matter between you and whoever sells it.

Data comes from somewhere else

Scores, clocks, schedules, odds and win probabilities all come from ESPN. ArenaSwap does not control that data, cannot guarantee it is accurate, complete, current or available, and has no say in when it changes. Data may be delayed, wrong or missing, and a stream usually runs behind the data feed. Do not treat anything in this extension as an official score, and do not bet on one.

PowerScore is an opinion

PowerScore estimates how exciting a game looks right now from public numbers. It is not a prediction, a probability, a betting model or a statement of fact, and it will sometimes send you away from the game you wanted to watch. That is what sensitivity, cooldown and favorite teams are for.
