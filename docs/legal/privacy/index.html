Privacy.

ArenaSwap has no account, no analytics and no ads. It has no server either, so there is nowhere for what you watch to go.

What the extension stores

Your settings and your tab assignments, on the computer you are using. That covers which leagues you follow, your favorite teams, sensitivity, cooldown and switch delay, your Standby Stream tab, any manual game boosts, and which tab you assigned to which game.

All of it stays on that computer. ArenaSwap never syncs any of it, so it does not reach your browser account or the other devices you sign into. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What it sends, and where

Two hosts, both ESPN's, and they are the only two the extension is permitted to reach:

  • site.api.espn.com for live scores, schedules, clocks, odds and win probabilities.
  • a.espncdn.com for team crests and league marks.

These are ordinary web requests, so ESPN sees your IP address and which league scoreboards ArenaSwap asked for, the same as if you had opened espn.com yourself. ArenaSwap attaches no identifier and no account to them. Turn a league off in Settings and it stops asking for that league at all.

There is no ArenaSwap server. No crash reporter, no telemetry, no usage pings, no A/B testing, and no third-party SDK of any kind.

The permissions it asks for

  • Tabs. To list your open tabs so you can assign games to them, and to switch, mute and unmute them. That grants the list of tabs and nothing more. None of ArenaSwap's code runs inside the pages you visit.
  • Storage. To keep the settings above.
  • Notifications. To tell you a switch happened. Your browser composes and shows them, and nothing leaves the machine.

On Firefox, ArenaSwap is listed as collecting no data at all, which is the strictest option Mozilla offers.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixel, and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs requests to its servers as part of hosting that. The fonts come from this site rather than from somebody else's server.

Two things here do reach ESPN from your browser. The demo screens load team crests froma.espncdn.com, and the PowerScore page scores real games, so it asks for live scoreboards while you have it open.

Children

ArenaSwap collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, which includes children under 13.

Changes

If this statement ever needs to change, the change ships with a version and that version's release notes say so. The date at the top is the last time anyone edited it.

Questions
