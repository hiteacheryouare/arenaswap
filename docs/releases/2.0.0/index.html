August 16, 2026 · Version 2.0.0

Every sport, one tab, twelve languages

ArenaSwap 2.0 rebuilds the scoring engine, grows from basketball to 31 leagues across six sports, replaces the settings screen with something you can find things in, and ships in twelve languages.

ArenaSwap started as a March Madness tool. 2.0 is the version where it stops being one.

31 leagues, six sports

Basketball, football, hockey, baseball, softball and soccer. That is the NBA, WNBA and both NCAA basketball tournaments; the NFL, NCAA Football and the UFL; the NHL and NCAA Men’s Hockey; MLB, NCAA Baseball, NCAA Softball and the World Baseball Classic; thirteen soccer competitions from the Premier League to Liga MX to both UEFA club cups; and seven Olympic competitions.

Every league you turn off stops being checked at all. Turn it back on and it returns to where it was in your list rather than to the bottom of it.

PowerScore, rebuilt

The number that decides where you get sent is now five signals, each with its own ceiling: closeness, late game, momentum, lead changes and comeback. On top of those sit boosts for your teams, the postseason and a live scoring chance, and a penalty for a game that has gone quiet.

Each sport gets its own idea of what “close” means. A one-goal hockey game and a one-point basketball game score alike; a one-run baseball game in the ninth outscores both. Open any game and the whole breakdown is there, signal by signal, with four charts showing how it got there.

A settings screen you can find things in

Twenty controls in one flat column became six categories with a search field over them. Every category fits in the popup without scrolling, except Leagues, which is 31 switches and a reorder list and has earned it.

Standby Stream

When every game you are tracking goes quiet, ArenaSwap can park you on a tab of your choosing instead of leaving you on whatever was least bad. Set it once in Settings.

Twelve languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Filipino, including the settings search keywords, so searching in your own language finds the setting you meant.

Also

  • Tabs are remembered between sessions, so reopening your streams does not mean reassigning them
  • Games in a delay or an intermission score zero, because nothing is happening
  • ArenaSwap works in Chrome, Firefox and Edge
