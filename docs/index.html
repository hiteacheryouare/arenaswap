Stop babysitting
your tabs.

Open your streams, assign each one to a game from the dropdown on its card, then walk away. When a better game turns up, ArenaSwap moves you to it, unmutes it, and mutes the one you left. No clicking, no checking, no missing.

Everything you're
watching, in one
panel.

Assigned tabs at the top, the rest of tonight underneath, and what starts next after that. Every card carries the live score, the situation, and the PowerScore that decides where you go. Nothing else to learn.

This is the popup, at the size it opens at, built from the same components the extension ships.

See why. Every time.

Open any game and you get the whole argument: how the score got there, how close it has been, and which of the five signals is doing the work right now. No black-box guessing.
