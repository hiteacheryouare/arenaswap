Set it once.
Watch everything.

Open your streams, assign them to games, walk away. ArenaSwap handles every switch from that point on.

  1. Open your streams.

    Pull up your games in separate browser tabs. Any streaming service that runs in a browser works with ArenaSwap.

  2. Assign each tab.

    Open the extension, find each game in the list, and link it to the right tab with a single dropdown.

  3. The best game finds you.

    ArenaSwap scores every live game and switches to the most exciting one automatically. You just watch.

Tuned in to you.

Sensitivity too high? Slow it down. Love your team but want the best game? Add a bonus. Going on a beer run? Standby Stream keeps something on screen. ArenaSwap works however you need it to.

How large a score gap needs to be before ArenaSwap switches.

Minimum time between switches. Stops rapid tab-flipping.

Switch delay

Waits before switching, for when your stream runs behind the data.

Favorite team bonus

Extra points for any game your teams are playing in.

Standby Stream

When every live game goes quiet, ArenaSwap parks you on a fallback tab you pick.
