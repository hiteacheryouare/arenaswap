The score behind the score.

ArenaSwap runs a live algorithm called PowerScore: a 100-point measure of how exciting a game is, right now.

Five independent signals feed into it. Games with frozen clocks (halftime, timeouts) take a penalty so ArenaSwap doesn't switch during a stoppage. Favorite team? Add a bonus.

Closeness(max 30 pts)
26

How tight is the margin? A tied game scores highest. Closeness counts for more as the game goes on, so an early nail-biter builds toward the final buzzer.

Late-Game Pressure(max 28 pts)
22

Tension rises steadily across the whole final period, but only when the game is close (a blowout in the final minute has none). There's an extra boost for tied games heading to overtime.

Momentum(max 28 pts)
24

Unanswered scoring runs shift the energy of a game. A run spikes this score, then it fades over the next few minutes.

Lead Changes(max 18 pts)
12

Back-and-forth games are more exciting than one-sided affairs. Multiple lead changes in recent history push this score to its ceiling.

Comeback(max 14 pts)
8

Is the trailing team clawing back? A big comeback always adds excitement to any game.

Standby Stream

Tuned in to you.

Sensitivity too high? Slow it down. Love your team but want the best game? Add a bonus. Going on a beer run? Standby Stream keeps something on screen. ArenaSwap works however you need it to.

  • Sensitivity (1–7)

    How large a score gap needs to be before ArenaSwap switches.

  • Switch Cooldown

    Minimum time between switches. Stops rapid tab-flipping.

  • Switch Delay

    Wait before switching. Useful when streams lag behind live data.

  • Favorite Team Bonus

    Extra points for games involving teams you care about.

  • Standby Stream

    When all live games go quiet, auto-switch to a designated fallback tab.

  • Game Boost

    Manually raise any game's PowerScore to lock it on screen a little longer.

30+ leagues across six sports: NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, MLS, EPL, Champions League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Liga MX, NCAAB, NCAAW, NCAAF, NCAA Hockey, WNBA, NWSL, NCAA Baseball, NCAA Softball, World Baseball Classic, FIFA World Cup, FIFA Women's World Cup, UFL, Europa League, and all relevant Olympic competitions.

ArenaSwap continuously scores every live game using PowerScore: a composite rating based on closeness, late-game pressure, momentum, lead changes, and comeback factor. When a game's PowerScore pulls meaningfully ahead of what you're watching, ArenaSwap switches your tab to it. The gap required is adjustable via sensitivity settings.

No. ArenaSwap is completely free, requires no account, and collects no personal data. Everything runs locally in your browser. Live scores come directly from ESPN's public API.

Yes. Any stream that runs in a browser tab works. This includes ESPN+, Peacock, Paramount+, YouTube TV, Hulu Live, fuboTV, and anything else browser-based.

PowerScore is a 0–100 scale combining five signals: Closeness, Late-Game Pressure, Momentum, Lead Changes, and Comeback Factor. Tension ramps toward the final buzzer, tied games get an overtime boost, and recent scoring spikes the score then fades. This means even low-scoring sports keep a moving graph. Games during stoppages (halftime, timeouts) receive a small penalty to avoid switching at the wrong moment.

Yes. You can unassign any tab from a game at any time. The extension immediately stops switching away from it. You can also lower the sensitivity setting to make switches less frequent overall.

Yes. When ArenaSwap switches to a game, it unmutes that tab and mutes all other assigned tabs so you always hear the right broadcast.

Yes. ArenaSwap is open source. The full codebase, including the PowerScore algorithm package, is on GitHub.
