Researched 2026-11-08 for the disrupted-games design (UI + PowerScore). Facts, not decisions yet.

- MLB: OBR 7.01(c) official after 5 (4.5 if home leads). Since 2020 a non-official called game is suspended, not wiped; tied official games suspend too (rule since 2007). Resume before next game between the clubs at that park, else at the opponent's park; called if no standings impact. Last tie: CHC-PIT 2016-09-29. Danny Jansen played for both sides of TOR@BOS (suspended 2024-06-26, resumed 2024-08-26). Doubleheaders 9 innings since 2022 (7-inning only 2020-21). 2022 = 42 rainouts (Retrosheet via weather.com); April 2025 had a record 28 postponements.
- MLS: suspended matches resume later from the stoppage minute with same players (FCD-STL 2023, CLT-CLB). 2015 policy: abandoned 76'+ result stands; current cutoff unverified.
- UEFA: suspended matches completed from the stoppage (PSG-Basaksehir Dec 2020 resumed from 14' next day); Nations League 2026/27 has "Completion of suspended matches" article.
- EPL replays abandoned matches from 0-0 (Luton-Bournemouth Dec 2023 replayed Mar 2024). DFB awards 2-0 (Bochum-Union Dec 2024); Liga MX/UEFA/FIFA award 3-0 (Querétaro-Atlas 2022).
- Lightning: 30 minutes after last strike within ~8 miles, clock resets each strike (NFL, NCAA, MLS, FIFA CWC 2025).

**How to apply:** treat "resume from the exact point" as the default everywhere except EPL/Bundesliga full replays; never assume a shortened final in soccer without a competition rule.
